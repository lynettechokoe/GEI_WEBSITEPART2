About Green Earth Initiative

Learn about our journey, mission, and the passionate team driving change.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Green Earth Initiative began as a small community group in Cape Town focused on local beach cleanups. Today, we've grown into a national movement with projects spanning tree planting, wildlife conservation, environmental education, and sustainable community development.

2015

Foundation

Started as a community beach cleanup initiative in Cape Town

2018

Expansion

Launched tree planting programs and expanded to Johannesburg

2022

National Reach

Became a registered NPO with projects in 3 provinces

Our Mission

To preserve and restore South Africa's natural environment through collaborative conservation efforts, education, and empowering communities to become stewards of their local ecosystems.

Our Vision

A sustainable future where communities and nature thrive together, and environmental conservation is at the heart of all development decisions.

Volunteer Opportunities

Tree Planting

Join our reforestation efforts every Saturday morning. No experience needed - we provide all tools and training!

  • When: Saturdays, 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  • Where: Various locations in Cape Town and Johannesburg
  • Commitment: Flexible - come when you can

Beach & River Cleanups

Help us tackle plastic pollution at its source. Monthly events across multiple locations.

  • When: First Saturday of each month
  • Where: Cape Town, Durban, and Gqeberha beaches
  • Provided: Gloves, bags, and cleaning equipment

Environmental Education

Assist with our school programs and community workshops. Perfect for those who love teaching.

  • When: Weekdays during school hours
  • Where: Local schools and community centers
  • Training: Provided for all volunteers
